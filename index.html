01 — OUR STORY

Built on the street,
worn around the world.

STÜSSY started as a signature on a surfboard — a name scrawled by hand that grew into a language spoken by skaters, surfers, DJs and artists across every timezone. We don't chase trends. We've been the trend since before it had a name.

Every piece we cut carries that same handwriting: unbothered, unmistakable, un-templated. Black and white, loud and quiet at the same time.
